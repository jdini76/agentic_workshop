# Weekly Marketing Brief SOP, version 1

Owner: Sarah Collins, Marketing Strategist

Purpose: prepare a traceable weekly planning artifact for human review without publishing content.

1. Confirm the employee and client profile versions used for the brief.
2. Normalize the requested week to Monday.
3. Separate approved facts from assumptions and missing information.
4. If required information is absent, plan information gathering instead of inventing content.
5. Map recommendations and future claims to source references.
6. Produce JSON and Markdown drafts with approval state `draft`.
7. Stop. A human reviewer must approve or request revision. Approval is not publication authority.

Quality checks:

- Every factual basis is present in the client profile's approved information.
- Every unresolved client-profile item appears in the brief's missing inputs.
- No public publishing action is assigned or performed.
- The structured artifact validates against `WeeklyMarketingBrief`.
